# ForgottenSouls

[Russian Version](README.ru.md)

A global difficulty add-on for **[Fargo's Souls Mod](https://github.com/Fargo-Team/FargowiltasSoulsMod)** on tModLoader. It adds a fourth difficulty mode, **"Sadomasochist"**, and heavily reworks vanilla Terraria. The whole point of the mod is built around this mode: it is meant to be a real challenge for experienced players.

> The mod is under active development. Some of what is described below may change.
> The version of the mod described here 0.1.9

## What the mod changes and adds

### World and progression

- **Hardmode ores** no longer come from Demon Altars. Cobalt or Palladium appears after the Wall of Flesh, Mythril or Orichalcum after the first mechanical boss, Adamantite or Titanium after the second.
- **Demon Altars** drop 3–5 Souls of Night when broken and summon two Wraiths.
- **Hallowed Bars** drop from mechanical bosses only after all three have been defeated.
- **The starting point** of the world is moved to the snow biome, and the first town NPC is the Demolitionist instead of the Guide.
- **A blizzard** rages constantly in the snow biome after Hardmode begins.
- **Sand tornadoes** appear in the desert during sandstorms.
- **Caves** slowly grow grass, on which sunflowers can sprout.
- **The Graveyard** raises monster spawn rate by 15% and applies Weak.
- **House raids** happen every 7 in-game days: monsters attack the town NPC homes, growing in number and strength each time (with limits).
- **Town NPCs** can arrive at night and move in instantly once a room is assigned. Town NPCs also have extra dialogue lines.
- **Recipes**: some vanilla recipes are reworked (for example, Night's Edge).
- **Music** for sub-biomes: Marble Cave, Granite Cave and Spider Nest.

### Player

- **Starting health is 50**, and each Life Crystal gives +15 health.
- **Class choice**: an item dropped by Moon Lord opens a window to pick a class (melee, ranged, magic, summoner) with bonuses and penalties.
- **Soul loss on death**: the death screen shows how many souls were lost.
- **Potion fatigue**: the more often you drink healing potions, the weaker they heal; fatigue wears off after some time without potions.
- **Weapon knockout**: with a 0.5% chance, a monster's body hit switches your active hotbar slot.
- **Grenades** deal 60% less damage.
- **Chests**: opening an untouched chest for the first time may apply a random debuff (chests placed by players are safe).
- **Fossil Enchant** (Fargo's Souls) no longer revives the player: bones on hit are kept, and it gains a chance to halve Potion Sickness and clear potion fatigue.

### The Observer

A special karma system. Good deeds (housing townsfolk, planting trees, cleansing world evil) please the Observer and grant stat bonuses. Bad deeds (killing innocent creatures and town NPCs, letting evil spread, graveyards) anger it: enemies grow stronger, lightning strikes the player, grenades rain from the sky, bosses show up on their own, Blood Moons and Solar Eclipses become more likely, and luck goes negative. The Observer's mood is shown by a buff icon that changes with it.

### Enemies and bosses

- **Boss memory**: the more often you kill the same boss, the better it predicts your movement. Each kill also makes the boss stronger (health, damage, defense, and more).
- **Monster dodging**: monsters have a chance to dodge attacks; nimble creatures (Tim and similar) always dodge the first attack, and stationary ones can always be hit.
- **Ghostly enemies** (Wraith, Ghost, Banshee) hunt light: they put out campfires, break torches, and switch your hotbar slot on touch.
- **Pirates and Skeleton Snipers** take cover and trade fire.
- **Skeleton Archers** fire 1 to 3 arrows per shot.
- **Worms** (Devourer, Bone Serpent) try to burst out right beneath the player.
- **Vampires** throw vampire daggers and heal on hit.
- **Swamp Thing** respawns right after death.
- **Crabs** grab the player by the legs and hold on.
- **Gnomes** steal money and run away.
- **Granite Elemental** fires a telegraphed sapphire bolt and can curl into a ball immune to weapons (explosions break through).
- **Trojan Squirrel** leaves explosions behind it while dashing in the first stage.
- **"The Snail"** crawls toward the player in Sadomasochist mode and kills on touch.

### Items and buffs

- **Multitool**: a weapon that fires 4–5 random throwable projectiles with effects.
- **"True" throwables** (snowball, shuriken, rotten egg, knives, star anise, javelins, bone, Molotov cocktail, dagger fish): never consumed, and apply effects on hit.
- **Adventurer Emblems** (ranks F, C, B, A), a starter bag, a cook enchantment, and more.
- **Buffs and debuffs**: Observer, Hero of the Village, Potion Fatigue, Firm Grip, Shattered Armor, Arachnophobia, Jammed, War Cry, and others.
